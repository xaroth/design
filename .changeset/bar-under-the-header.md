New `Bar` part for a bar under the header: navigation at the start (`start`), fields right aligned at the end (`end`), a line under it, and a polite live region under the bar for one short `notice`. The live region is always rendered, so a notice that appears or changes later is announced. Below 720px of its own width the fields come first, on as many lines as they need, and the navigation under them.
